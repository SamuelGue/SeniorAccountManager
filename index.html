WHAT MY WEEK ACTUALLY LOOKS LIKE

How I'd turn account data into action. The week below repeats inside the 30-day cycle further down. It's how I'd run the account, not something I've already done on it.

MONDAY / DATA

FIND THE SIGNAL

Find the signal before deciding what the problem is.

  • Pull creator and funnel numbers: views, clicks, consultations, paid conversions, revenue and CAC, by creator and by piece of content.
  • Check where the biggest drop-off is, using the Section 03 diagnostic ranges: Views → Clicks, Clicks → Consultation, Consultation → Paid.
  • Tag the content: who gets attention, who gets intent, which story angles get clicks, and whether it's all coming from a handful of creators.
  • Low Views → Clicks points me to content. Healthy clicks but few consultations points me to the landing page and offer. Healthy consultations but low paid points me to fit and the conversion experience.

OUTPUT“One clear thing worth investigating + the evidence behind it.”

TUESDAY / ACTION

TURN THE SIGNAL INTO ACTION

The brief comes straight from Monday's evidence.

  • Pick the creator or content pattern worth developing, and decide what actually needs to change.
  • Write two focused creator briefs. Each one carries a hypothesis, such as repeat a story angle that got clicks, change the hook, answer a common objection or tighten the CTA.
  • If Monday says the drop is after the click, the brief is about the route after the click, not more content.
  • Agree the one metric that decides whether each test worked, before it goes live.

OUTPUT“Two focused tests ready to run.”

WEDNESDAY / ALIGNMENT

CLIENT / CREATIVE ALIGNMENT

A conversation, not a presentation of a finished answer.

  1. What changed since last time.
  2. What the data suggests.
  3. What I think we should test. My view, open to being wrong.
  4. What I don't know yet.
  5. What decision or input I need from Skin + Me and Tano.

OUTPUT“Aligned next action + clear owner + what we'll measure.”
